What we fix

Where activity venue websites lose bookings.

We found these problems again and again when we audited venue sites.

AreaThe problemWhat we do
Booking"Call us to book"Online booking on every page, so evening and weekend browsers can book while they're on the site.
PricesPrices buried or out of dateOne clear price list with a Book button on each row.

FAQ

Questions venue owners ask.

Do I have to change my booking system?

No. We design the site around the system you already use and link every Book button straight into it.

Is the mock-up really free?

Yes. If you don't want to go ahead, you keep the mock-up and owe us nothing.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Prices, session times, offers and photos are all editable without touching code. We show you how at launch.

What do you need from me?

Your current prices, your best photos, and access to your domain and booking system. We handle the rest.

Who are you?

KOLABS Agency is a small, new team that is part of KOLABS, a community of builders. We're early, which is why our first five clients get founding prices and direct access to the people building their site.
