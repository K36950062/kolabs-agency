FAQ

Questions venue owners ask.

Do I have to change my booking system?

No. We design the site around the system you already use and link every Book button straight into it.

Is the mock-up really free?

Yes. If you don't want to go ahead, you keep the mock-up and owe us nothing.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Prices, session times, offers and photos are all editable without touching code. We show you how at launch.

What do you need from me?

Your current prices, your best photos, and access to your domain and booking system. We handle the rest.
