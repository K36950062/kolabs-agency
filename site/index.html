FAQ

Questions business owners ask.

Do I have to change my booking system?

No. We connect your new site to the booking system you already use. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you choose one that fits your business.

Is the mock-up really free?

Yes. Book a call and we'll bring a mock-up of your new homepage. If you decide not to go ahead, you keep it and owe us nothing.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, with the Complete package. It includes easy editing and a training session. On Starter and Growth, the £50-a-month care plan covers your changes, so you just tell us what to update.

What's not included in the price?

A few things are paid directly to other companies: your domain name, any booking software subscription, and card payment fees when customers pay online. We'll tell you upfront which ones apply to you.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Starter or Growth and add booking, events or editing later. You only pay the difference in the work involved.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, your best photos, your prices, and access to your domain and booking system. We handle everything else.
