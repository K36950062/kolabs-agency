The colour tells you how hard it is

Every route uses holds of one colour. Start on yellow and work your way up. Most people climb their first teal within a month.

    Big holds, like climbing a ladder. Your first visit.

  • Teal

    Smaller holds and your first real moves.

  • Blue

    Overhangs. Technique starts to matter.

  • Pink

    Tricky sequences for regular climbers.

  • Black

    Our hardest. Strong fingers only.
