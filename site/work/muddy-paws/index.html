How a groom works

Most grooms take two to three hours. Your dog is never left in a crate.

  1. Drop off

    Tell us about anything sensitive, like ears or paws, and any skin conditions.

  2. Pamper

    Warm bath, gentle dry, brush out, cut and a nail trim. Treats along the way.

  3. Pick up

    We text you a photo when they're done, usually with a bandana on.

Questions

Anything else, just text us.

My dog is nervous. Is that OK?

Completely. We only ever have one dog in at a time, so it's quiet. We can also do a short "meet and treat" visit first for £10.

How often should I book?

Every six to eight weeks for most coats. Doodles and poodles are happiest every four to six.

Do you handle matted coats?

Yes, gently. If it would hurt to brush out, we'll call you and suggest a shorter clip instead.

What if I need to cancel?

Free with 24 hours' notice. After that it's half the price, unless your dog is poorly.
